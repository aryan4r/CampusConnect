About CampusConnect

One place for every event at Lovely Professional University.

Our mission

Big campuses have plenty going on, but posters, group chats and word of mouth leave many students finding out too late. CampusConnect puts every event, with its date, venue and price, on one clear page.

Our story

CampusConnect began as a simple idea: a student should be able to find an event and register in under a minute. It also gives club organizers a single place to reach the whole campus.

Frequently asked questions

For students and club organizers.

How do I register for an event?

Open the Events page, press Register on any event, and fill in your name, registration number, email and the event.

What is my registration number format?

Use your 8-digit LPU registration number, for example 1260xxxx.

Do I have to pay to join?

Many events are free. Paid events show their ticket price on the card, such as ₹199 for Innovate 2026.

How can my club list an event?

Use the form on this page. Share the event name, date, venue and price, and we will get back to you.

Can I register for more than one event?

Yes. Submit the form once for each event you want to attend.
